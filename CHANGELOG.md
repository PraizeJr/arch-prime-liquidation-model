# Changelog

Every correction to the model or its inputs is logged here, with the date and who raised it.

## v0.1 (initial release)
- Deterministic liquidation and shortfall model built on Arch Prime's published mechanics.
- Inputs split into documented, assumed and unverified (see `data/assumptions.csv`).
- Outputs: two charts in `figures/` and a scenario table in `data/`.
